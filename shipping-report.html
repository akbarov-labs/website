Staging for akbarovlabs.uz

Updated recommendation: drop it. You were right, and I’m changing my earlier advice.

  • It would match your other apps. 9 of your 16 deployed apps run production-only, including Posbon, which dropped staging on 5 Nov.
  • The manual step already causes drift. The site’s newest commit (4198f71, 28 Oct) has sat on staging for 8 days without reaching production. Your personal site has the same gap, with production stuck at 14 Oct.
  • The risk is low. It is a static site with no database, it can be checked locally with Docker, and promote.yml can roll back to any recent image.

The change is the one Posbon already made: push to main deploys straight to production. Removing the staging stack on the server is a separate manual step. Add staging back when the contact form and AI assistant arrive.

Main products

  • Kitoblarim (78 merged PRs): all 8 roadmap phases, audiobooks and monthly recaps. New since October: shareable monthly reading lists, a news-channel post of everyone’s month after admin approval, and reviews written in the chat from Reads.

  • Aktiv Yodla: 500 seeded words, spaced repetition, a landing page at / with the Mini App at /app, admin stats and broadcasts, and a scheduled content backup.
